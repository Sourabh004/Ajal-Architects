/ 03 — Design Manifesto

How we
draw

Five positions the studio returns to on every project, from a 40 sq m apartment to a campus.

  1. 01

    Climate first, form second

    Orientation, shade and cross-ventilation are decided before a single elevation is drawn. Comfort should be structural, not mechanical.

  2. 02

    Material honesty

    Concrete reads as concrete, brick as brick. We do not clad one material to imitate another — surfaces are allowed to weather in public.

  3. 03

    The plan is the argument

    If it does not resolve in plan, no amount of rendering will save it. We test circulation, light and privacy in orthographic drawings first.

  4. 04

    Detail at 1:1

    Junctions, reveals and thresholds are drawn full size. The quality of a building is decided in the twenty millimetres nobody photographs.

  5. 05

    Build for the second owner

    Spaces are planned so they can change use without demolition. Longevity is the most effective form of sustainability we know.

/ 04 — Our Story

Eleven years, one obsession

2014

A table and two drawing boards

Ajal Architects opens in a converted garage in Jayanagar with one commission — a 1,200 sq ft house for a retired schoolteacher.

2017

First public building

A community library in Tumakuru puts the studio's low-embodied-carbon detailing in front of a wider audience for the first time.

2020

The material library

We start cataloguing every finish we specify — supplier, embodied carbon, ageing behaviour. It now runs to 400+ entries and drives every spec.

2023

Studio of fourteen

The practice grows to fourteen architects across design, technical and site teams — still with one drawing review table at the centre of the room.

2025

Beyond the city

Work extends into Kerala and coastal Karnataka, with three hospitality projects testing how the studio's language handles humidity and salt.
